## 2026-10-10 — Initial discovery

### Competition

- The official [2026 Space Apps page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/) lists the theme **The Next Frontier**, event dates **November 14–15, 2026**, registration, and teams of no more than six.
- An official local-event schedule reports challenge summaries on September 17 and full statements/resources on October 28.
- That local-event guidance also says intended solutions are started, built, and submitted during the hackathon. This requires confirmation with the chosen Local Lead before any implementation.
- The official challenge-summary text found on team pages confirms **Space Mission Design Game** and names mission objectives, spacecraft design, instruments, launch vehicles, budgets, power, mass, communications, and orbital constraints.

### Prior winners and judging

- The [2024 awards page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/nasa-space-apps-2024/awards/) documents global award definitions.
- Official local-event pages describe the recurring judging criteria as impact, creativity, validity, relevance, and presentation.
- The [2023 winners announcement](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/blog/the-nasa-space-apps-challenge-live-global-winners-announcement/) shows that data-driven interactive experiences and games have won Best Use of Science and Best Storytelling. Team Voyagers from Bangladesh is a particularly relevant precedent.
- The [2024 winners page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/nasa-space-apps-2024/awards/global-winners/) states that ten winners were selected from more than 9,900 submissions, illustrating the need for ruthless differentiation and presentation clarity.

### Science/data

- MOLA MEGDR provides global topographic maps produced by binning altimetry over the Mars Global Surveyor mission and is the strongest base for a terrain mechanic.
- NAIF SPICE is the authoritative NASA/JPL observation-geometry system suitable for ephemeris and communications geometry.
- OSDR provides space-life-science and radiation datasets, but measurements must be kept in mission context.
- A NASA technical Mars mission source emphasizes that oxygen can support propulsion/life support/EVA and water can support life support, EVA, shielding, and propellant production—useful for coupled-resource gameplay.

### Strategic conclusion

The idea is a direct challenge match, but the initial request is far beyond a 48-hour build. The competitive version should be a polished vertical slice with a complete mission story and unusually strong provenance, then expand after submission.
