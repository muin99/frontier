# Expedition redesign

User explicitly requested a new visual direction, 4 November 2026.

Hero target: NASA's detailed Z2 suit, textile seams, dirt, boots, rigid torso and dark visor; a normalized articulated runtime derivative, not primitive body blocks. Crew: detailed textured MakeHuman variants, distinct faces/hair, restrained flight uniforms and clean skin/hair materials; remove rigid coat panels.

World: six distinct procedural districts in a 1.5 km arena. Basalt columns and dark lava pavement; ochre dunes with ripples; layered canyon walls; pale sulfate mesas; cold highland ridges; terraced impact rim. Preserve MOLA regional relief beneath authored local features. Place recognisable distant silhouettes, changing paths and seeded discoveries. No trees, liquid rivers, terrestrial rain or invented NASA mineral detections.

Gameplay: two traversal tools in the pack, trade-offs for sand, cold and steep terrain; inspect/scan/cache/weather-station tasks; refuges and varied exploration rewards. Seed and generator version saved. Regional terrain and local geology combinations are simulated analogs, not a measured contiguous Martian location.

Lighting: restrained physical material response, warm directional light, cool ambient fill, dust varying visibility and sky saturation. Mission control: graphite consoles, tiered work areas, task lighting, large trajectory display, amber warning channels and clear walkways. Native 1280×720 inspection required.

Data: source PDS MOLA + calibrated MEDA profiles; weather events are seeded, time-compressed scenarios. Preserve labels, checksums, measured units, quality flags and limitations.
